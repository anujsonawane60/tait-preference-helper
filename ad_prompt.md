# Advertisement prompt — TAIT preference-filling service

Fill in the four bracketed values, paste the whole block into ChatGPT, and it will write the
ad in Marathi. A ready-made draft is at the bottom if you want to skip straight to posting.

---

## The prompt (copy from here)

```
You are writing a promotional advertisement in MARATHI for a service that helps
TAIT 2025 candidates in Maharashtra decide their school preference order.

MY DETAILS — use these exactly:
- Name: [YOUR NAME]
- WhatsApp number: [YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER]
- Fee: [YOUR FEE, or write "मोफत" if free]
- Delivery time: [e.g. "२४ तासांत"]

BACKGROUND — this is what the service actually does:
A TAIT candidate downloads a "Generated Preferences" PDF from the Pavitra portal
(पवित्र प्रणाली). It lists every school they are eligible for — often 500 to 900 schools.
To decide which school to put first, second, third and so on, the candidate would have to
open each school's separate advertisement PDF and read two tables inside it. That is many
hours of work and most candidates never do it properly.

I have built software that reads all 2,400+ school advertisement PDFs at once. The
candidate sends me only their own Generated Preferences PDF, and I send back one Excel
file, named after them, containing every school on their list with:
  - the number of vacant posts in THEIR subject at each school
  - the number of posts in THEIR category (SC / ST / OBC / EWS / SEBC / VJ-A / NT-B /
    NT-C / NT-D / SBC / खुला) at that school
  - posts under the parallel quotas (महिला, खेळाडू, माजी सैनिक)
  - the school's total posts, and what percentage belongs to their category
  - sorted so the schools with the most opportunity for them appear at the top

AUDIENCE: Marathi-speaking teachers and B.Ed / D.Ed graduates across Maharashtra,
mostly reading on a phone via WhatsApp. Many are anxious and short of time.

TONE: warm, direct, practical, respectful. Speak to one person, not a crowd. No hype,
no exclamation-heavy sales voice, no fake urgency, no "last chance" pressure.

MUST INCLUDE:
- A short opening line naming the real problem (too many schools, no time to check each one)
- What the candidate receives, as a scannable list with emoji ticks
- That they only need to send their Generated Preferences PDF — nothing else
- Fee and delivery time
- WhatsApp number as the single call to action

MUST NOT DO — this matters, keep it honest:
- Do NOT promise a job, a posting, or selection. The service organises published
  information; it does not influence any outcome.
- Do NOT claim to be official, government-approved, or connected to the Pavitra portal
  or the Education Department.
- Do NOT invent statistics, success rates, or testimonials.
- Do NOT say the tool reveals which category a particular subject post belongs to. The
  advertisement PDFs publish subject-wise vacancies and a school-wide reservation roster
  as two separate tables, and never link them. The Excel shows both numbers side by side.
- End with one plain honest line: the data is taken from the official advertisement PDFs,
  and this is an information service only.

LENGTH: under 150 Marathi words. It must fit in one WhatsApp message without scrolling
much.

Now give me FOUR versions:
1. WhatsApp / Telegram group message (the main one)
2. A very short WhatsApp Status or Instagram Story version — 40 words maximum
3. A Facebook post version — slightly longer, may include a short line about why I built it
4. Poster text — a bold headline, 4 to 5 bullet points, and contact details at the bottom

Write everything in natural spoken Marathi, not translated-sounding Marathi. Use the
English words that Marathi teachers actually use in conversation — Excel, PDF, WhatsApp,
TAIT, पसंतीक्रम — rather than forcing pure Marathi equivalents.
```

## Notes before you post

**Keep the last line.** It costs you nothing and protects you. Candidates under pressure
read fast, and if anyone later believes you promised a posting, that one line is what
settles it.

**Say "both numbers side by side", never "your subject in your category".** The source PDFs
genuinely do not contain that cross-tab — subject-wise vacancies and the reservation roster
are separate tables. Promising the combination is the one claim your own data can't back.

**Ask for the PDF, not personal details.** You need nothing except the Generated Preferences
file. Not their seat number, marks, Aadhaar, or portal password — and saying so plainly in
the ad makes people more willing to contact you, not less.

**About their files.** You'll be receiving documents that identify real candidates. Delete
them once you've sent the Excel back, and don't forward anyone's file to anyone else.
